🧼 Cat Parent Guide

Essential Cat Care Hub

Keep your fluffy companions healthy, happy, and purring with these expert care guides.

Nutrition & Water

  • High-protein meat-based diet
  • Fresh water fountains to encourage hydration
  • Avoid chocolate, onions, & dairy milk

Grooming & Hygiene

  • Daily brushing for long-haired cats
  • Clean litter box daily
  • Regular nail trimming & dental wipes

Playtime & Enrichment

  • 15-30 mins interactive play daily
  • Scratching posts to save furniture
  • Cozy sunbathing perches near windows

Health & Vet Care

  • Annual veterinary health checkups
  • Core vaccinations & flea prevention
  • Spay/Neuter for healthy longevity
